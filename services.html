What Michel Offers

Culinary guidance shaped by precision, discipline, and operational reality

Michel's consulting work is built for restaurants that need more than surface-level advice. The focus is on building kitchens that perform with clarity, rhythm, and consistency — while preserving the culinary identity behind them.
