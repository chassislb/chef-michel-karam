The Consultant

A career shaped by real kitchens, real pressure, and real results

Michel Karam started his professional career in 2000 and has spent 25 years working inside some of the most demanding hospitality environments in the region — across Lebanon, Bahrain, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Iraq, Malaysia, and beyond.

He has led kitchens through pre-openings, restructured operations under pressure, built brigades from scratch, and developed menus that work not just on paper but in the reality of a full service. His experience spans restaurants, resorts, diplomatic institutions, and multi-outlet operations.

Today he works directly with restaurant and bar owners in Lebanon — bringing that experience into their kitchens through one-visit consultations, menu audits, and operational restructuring that produces clear, measurable results.

Culinary Philosophy

Great kitchens are built on discipline, respect, and consistency

Michel's philosophy begins with respect for ingredients, technique, and the quiet rigor of the craft itself. Creativity has value, but it reaches its fullest expression only when supported by order, discipline, and a clear standard in every detail.

For him, excellence in cuisine is never limited to taste alone. It is found in the rhythm of service, the composure of the brigade, the strength of communication, and the ability of a kitchen to perform with calm confidence under pressure.

This balance between culinary sensitivity and operational control defines his work — kitchens that feel precise without rigidity, refined without excess, and capable of delivering consistency at the highest level.
